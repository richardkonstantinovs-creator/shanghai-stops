A place page tells you where to go. A route tells you what order to go in, which exit to leave by, when to arrive so the queue is short, how much to order at each stop so you are not full by the third, and what to do when one of them is shut.

  • A cheap day 5 stops · €6

  • A rainy day 5 stops · €6

    Five stops, four of them indoors and the fifth under a covered arcade, on a day when Shanghai is doing its worst.

  • A late night 4 stops · €6

    The last coffee, a tea house at dusk, the river at blue hour, and wontons at half past eight.

  • Breakfast in Shanghai 5 stops · €6

    Five breakfasts, across five mornings, that will change what you think this city eats.
