Places · Hongkou 虹口区

Sanjiaodi Market

三角地菜场

Free, and one of the best hours you can spend in this city. Go at seven in the morning.

Market free nerve 3 · real

012345 5 stops · worth the ride

What to order

No order card for this one yet. When there is, it will be here, free.

A working wet market on three floors. Live fish on the ground floor, vegetables and tofu in the middle, dry goods and a barber upstairs.

Nothing here is for you and that is the point. Nobody performs, nobody sells you anything, and if you stand still for ten minutes you will watch four people argue about the price of a fish and settle it happily.

It costs nothing. Take the stairs to the top and come down slowly. Don’t photograph faces without asking — a nod and a raised camera is enough of a question and people will tell you.
