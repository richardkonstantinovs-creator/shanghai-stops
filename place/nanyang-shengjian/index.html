Bite a corner off first and let it cool for five seconds. I did not do this the first time and I could not taste anything else for two days.

Fourteen yuan for four. The curried beef soup for eight is thin and orange and looks like nothing and you should get it anyway; it is what the buns are meant to be eaten with.

There is nowhere to sit. There are two chest-high ledges along the wall and everyone eats facing the street. This is the correct way to do it.
