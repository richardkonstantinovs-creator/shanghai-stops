Places · Yangpu 杨浦区

Banceng Books

半层书店

A bookshop up a staircase with almost nothing in English, and worth the climb anyway.

Bookshop free nerve 2 · mild

0123 3 stops · if you're there

What to order

No order card for this one yet. When there is, it will be here, free.

Half a floor, which is what the name means. You climb a staircase that feels like it belongs to a flat, and there is a bookshop in it.

Almost everything is in Chinese. There is a small shelf of translated fiction and a table of design and photography books that are worth looking at whatever you read.

Three stops. It is a nice half hour if you are in Yangpu, and Yangpu is not somewhere most people go. Coffee is available and not the reason to be there.
