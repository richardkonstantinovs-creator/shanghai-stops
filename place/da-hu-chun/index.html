This is the famous old chain, and it is genuinely old — the recipe uses a leavened dough rather than the thin unleavened kind, so the bun is bready and soaks up more of the juice.

It is a seven because it is reliable and central and I would send someone here who is nervous and short on time. If you have an afternoon and any appetite for a lane, go to Nanyang instead.

The prices on the card were checked a while ago and I would treat them as approximate. That is what the date under them is for.
