Practical guides

How to pay for things, how to get online, how the metro works, how to get a taxi to understand you. Free, never gated, and every one of them carries the date it was last checked at the top rather than the bottom.

Rules for foreigners using Chinese payment apps change often, and a wrong instruction here means someone lands in Shanghai unable to pay for anything. So the build refuses to publish a guide that has not been walked through in the last four months. If a page is missing below, that is why, and it is the correct behaviour.

Nothing here yet, on purpose.

Six guides are written — payments, eSIM and data, the metro, taxis, delivery, and what works with no Chinese bank account. None of them has been walked through end to end since it was written, and this site does not publish travel instructions that nobody has checked.

They go up as they are verified, one at a time, with the date and the name of whoever checked them at the top of the page.

In the meantime: the places are all free and all real, and the scale explains what a number means.
