A cheap day

Breakfast, a lane, a market and a proper lunch for forty-three yuan a head, all in.

Places
5
Riding
22 min
Walking
3.1 km
Elapsed
06:30 to about 13:00
Spend
¥43 a head

Who this is not for Not for anyone who wants to sit down for long. Three of these five have no chairs at all, and one has no shop.

Forty-three yuan is about five euros. That is the entire food budget for this day and it is not a stunt — it is roughly what the people who live on these streets spend.

The three cheapest stops here are the two markets and the lane, and they are free. That is the trick to eating cheaply in this city: the expensive part is sitting down, and half of what is worth seeing does not involve sitting down at all.

Everything is in two districts and the riding is twenty-two minutes total.

  1. 1

    Stop 1 arrive 06:30–07:30

    She stops when the pot is empty, which is usually about half nine but has been eight. Early is not optional here.

    Lane Wonton 弄堂小馄饨

    10 stops · Jing'an · nerve 4

    What to order here

    • One bowl of 小馄饨, twelve yuan. One 粢饭糕, three. Soy milk, three.
    • Eighteen yuan. Do not add anything — you are eating four more times today.
    • Cash. Actually cash. Bring a twenty and a ten.

    To stop 2

    Walk 7 min. back out of the lane, left, then the arched entrance between the two shops

    If it's closed

    The wonton stall inside the Changping Road market, two minutes further on — Indoors, open until eleven, and eleven yuan.

A spare hour

Walk the Hongkou lanes west of the stadium. Nobody goes and there is nothing to buy, which is the point.

Where to go on after dark

You are in Hongkou with money left. Wanshou Zhai reopens at four and does the big wontons until seven.
