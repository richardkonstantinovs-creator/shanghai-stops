A first day

Off a European flight, awake at five, and every good thing eaten before the crowds arrive.

Who this is not for Not for a second visit — three of these are on every list. And not if you want a lie-in: the whole point is being out before seven.

This is the day I give people who land at Pudong in the morning, sleep badly, and are awake at half four with nothing to do.

It is built around one fact: everything worth eating in this city happens early, and the jet lag that ruins a European’s first day here is the only time you will ever be up for it. You are awake at five whether you like it or not. Use it.

The riding is short — forty minutes in total, most of it on one line — because a first day should not be spent underground.

  1. Stop 1 arrive 06:00–07:15

    You are awake anyway. Between five thirty and seven thirty the park is a different city, and by nine it is an ordinary park.

    Lu Xun Park at six 鲁迅公园晨练

    3 stops · Hongkou · nerve 1

    What to order here

    • Nothing. Buy a bottle of water at the gate and walk the loop anticlockwise.
    • Stand and watch the choir by the pond for ten minutes. It is the thing you will remember.

    To stop 2

    Walk 9 min. leave by the south gate, cross at the lights, then the market street on the left

    If it's closed

    Fuxing Park, six stops away on Line 10 — The same thing happens there, slightly smaller and slightly later — from about six thirty.

A spare hour

Walk the lanes behind the Bund — the block between Sichuan Road and Jiangxi Road is still ordinary offices and laundry, four minutes from the promenade.

Where to go on after dark

Erguang Wonton is open until nine and is two stops away on Line 9. That is the correct end to this day: shrimp wontons, standing up, in a room with the lights on too bright.
